### Reproducibility finding
Re-running `src.analysis` on the frozen per-frame data reproduces the repeatability, reference and
anomaly tables **byte-for-byte**, but RQ2 differs in the 3rd decimal (raw 14.297 vs 14.301 px,
corrected 10.339 vs 10.348 px) and one recording has one more usable frame. Cause: the per-frame
tables were regenerated by a later pipeline run (16:16–16:18) after the published analysis (16:00), and
the pipeline is **not bit-exact** between runs (no OpenCV RNG seed for GrabCut; multi-threaded
inference). Both result sets are kept in the baseline (`results/` = as published,
`results_rerun_from_frozen_frames/`).

### Other problems found
* **Dependencies:** `requirements.txt` (Stage 1) lacks scipy/scikit-learn; two Python environments
  (3.13 for the audit, 3.12 for everything else) — unify on 3.12. `sounddevice` is an unused transitive
  dependency of MediaPipe.
* **Unverified assumptions still in code/docs:** 160×120 native sensor (spec only); the openness
  threshold (1.88) and rig shift (−12.3, −31.5 px) are learned from this dataset and will not transfer
  to another camera or protocol.
* **Scientific limitations** (all ten listed in the prompt) are confirmed and stay in every report.
